# Service Page Copy Format

Owned by: `copy-structure`

---

## Purpose

Individual service pages have two jobs: rank for service-specific queries and convert visitors who arrive already interested. The page must contain enough specific content to satisfy both a human reading it and an AI system scanning it for quotable facts.

A thin service page — one that just names the service and adds a CTA — does neither. It will not rank, it will not be cited by AI, and it will not convert a visitor who has questions.

---

## Required Page Structure

Every individual service page must have these sections, in this order:

### 1. H1 — Service Name + Location
```
Kitchen Remodeling in Portland, OR
```
Same formula as the hero. If the page is city-specific, the city goes in the H1.

### 2. Opening Description (2–3 sentences)
What the service is. Who it is for. What the result looks like.

Not: "We offer kitchen remodeling services to homeowners throughout the Portland area."  
Yes: "Full kitchen renovations for Portland homeowners — from demolition and framing to custom cabinets, countertops, and final finish work. We handle structural changes, electrical, and plumbing coordination so you're dealing with one contractor, not five."

The opening description must answer: what exactly is this, and why would someone hire you for it?

### 3. What's Included

A specific list of what the service covers. Use bullet points or a short list.

```md
## What's Included

- Demo and haul-away of existing cabinets and countertops
- New cabinet installation (custom or semi-custom)
- Countertop fabrication and installation
- Backsplash tile installation
- Under-cabinet lighting
- Appliance placement and connection
- Final punch list and cleanup
```

Rules:
- Must name real items, not "all your needs"
- If something is commonly expected but NOT included, add a "What's Not Included" or note: "Does not include appliance purchase — we install, not supply."

### 4. Pricing

The most-read section on any service page. Be as specific as the client permits.

**Preferred:** Starting price + range
```
## Pricing

Kitchen remodels start at $18,000 for standard-size kitchens with semi-custom cabinets. Full custom projects with layout changes typically range from $35,000–$65,000+.

Free estimates are available — contact us for an on-site assessment.
```

**If client declines specific pricing:**
```
## Pricing

Pricing depends on kitchen size, cabinet selection, and scope of structural work. We provide free on-site estimates. Most Portland homeowners budget between $20,000 and $60,000 for a full kitchen remodel.

Contact us to schedule your free estimate.
```

Never omit pricing entirely. "Call for pricing" with no other guidance is a page that does not get cited by AI and does not build trust with human visitors.

### 5. Process / Timeline

How does the job actually get done? How long does it take?

```md
## Our Process

1. **Free estimate** — We visit your home, assess the space, and provide a written quote within 48 hours.
2. **Design and materials** — You select cabinets, countertops, and finishes. We coordinate ordering and delivery.
3. **Demo** — Typically 1–2 days. We handle haul-away.
4. **Build** — Cabinet installation, countertop templating and install, backsplash, electrical finish work.
5. **Punch list and cleanup** — Final walkthrough with you before sign-off.

Most kitchen remodels take 3–6 weeks from demo to completion, depending on scope and material lead times.
```

### 6. FAQ Section

Minimum 5 questions. See `faq-format.md` for question selection rules.

The FAQ section is the highest-value section for AI citation. Every real question with a direct answer is a candidate for an AI-generated response that cites this page.

```md
## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does a kitchen remodel take?

Most kitchen remodels take 3–6 weeks from demo to final walkthrough. Larger projects with custom cabinetry may take 8–10 weeks, as custom cabinet lead times are typically 4–6 weeks.

### Do you handle permits?

Yes. We pull all required permits for structural, electrical, and plumbing work. Permit costs are included in the project quote.

...
```

### 7. Service Area

Name the cities. Not "surrounding areas."

```md
## Service Area

We serve Portland, Beaverton, Hillsboro, Tigard, Lake Oswego, Tualatin, and surrounding Washington County communities.
```

### 8. CTA

One primary CTA at the bottom. Same as the hero or page-appropriate.

```md
## Ready to Start?

Contact us for a free on-site estimate. We'll assess your kitchen and provide a written quote within 48 hours.

[Get a Free Estimate] — primary button
Or call: (503) 555-1234
```

## Related Services (Optional but Recommended)

Link to 2–3 related services. Internal links help both crawlers and visitors.

```md
## Related Services

- [Bathroom Remodeling](/services/bathroom-remodeling)
- [Basement Finishing](/services/basement-finishing)
- [Whole-Home Renovation](/services/whole-home-renovation)
```

---

## Length

A service page with all required sections will typically be 600–1,200 words. This is appropriate. Thin pages under 300 words should not exist.

Do not pad with filler to hit a word count. Every sentence must carry information.

---

## Service Page Copy Checklist

- [ ] H1 states the service name and location
- [ ] Opening description says what the service is and who it's for (2–3 sentences)
- [ ] What's Included section lists specific items
- [ ] Pricing section is present with real numbers or an honest "pricing varies" explanation
- [ ] Process section explains how the job works and how long it takes
- [ ] At least 5 FAQ pairs with direct answers
- [ ] Service area names specific cities
- [ ] One primary CTA at the bottom
- [ ] Links to at least 2 related service pages
- [ ] No adjective-only claims anywhere on the page
- [ ] No invented pricing or statistics
