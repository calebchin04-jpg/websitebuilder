## 9. Strategic Website Direction

[3–6 sentences defining the strategic intent of the site. This is not a visual direction statement — it is a strategic one. It should answer: What problem does this site solve for the visitor? What must the visitor believe after 10 seconds on this site? What is the site's job in the client's overall sales process — is it the primary lead generation tool, a trust confirmation step, a conversion engine for already-warm prospects, or something else?]

**Positioning translated into site behavior:**
- [Positioning claim → how the site demonstrates it structurally. E.g., "We're technique-first → the hero leads with before/after proof, not a generic service description."]
- [Differentiator → structural expression. E.g., "Guaranteed re-clean → a visible guarantee callout near the primary CTA."]
- [Trust gap → mitigation approach. E.g., "Only 12 reviews → founder story and process explanation carry more weight than review count."]

## 10. User Journey

[Describe the path a typical visitor takes from first landing to conversion. This is a flow description, not a page list.]

**Journey stages:**

1. **Arrival:** [How the visitor lands — search, referral, Google Maps, social. What state are they in? Are they comparison-shopping, in urgency, or doing research?]

2. **Orientation:** [First 5–10 seconds. What must the visitor understand immediately? What question must the site answer first? What is the "above the fold" job?]

3. **Trust building:** [How the visitor starts to believe this is the right choice. What proof does the site present, in what order, and why?]

4. **Evaluation:** [How the visitor examines the offer more closely — services, pricing, team, process. What information do they need before they're ready to act?]

5. **Conversion:** [The moment they decide to act. What reduces friction? What makes the CTA feel safe and easy to take?]

6. **Post-conversion:** [What happens after they submit a form or call? Is there a thank-you page? Is there a confirmation? What impression should they leave with?]
