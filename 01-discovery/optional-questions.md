# Optional Questions

Ask these questions after all required questions are answered. Do not ask all of them — scan the existing brief and ask only the ones that are missing and would meaningfully improve the output.

Group optional questions into a single batch. Keep this pass brief.

---

## When to Ask Each One

### O.1 Brand voice and messaging tone

Ask if: the brief lacks any indication of how the client communicates or what their copy personality should feel like.

> How does this business talk to its customers? Is the tone formal or conversational? Authoritative or friendly? Technical or plain-language? Are there any phrases, words, or styles that feel wrong for this brand?

Skip if: the business type and audience already make this obvious, or if the user has provided copy samples.

---

### O.2 Competitor analysis depth

Ask if: no competitors have been named and the business is in a competitive local market.

> Who are 2–3 direct local competitors? What do their websites do well that yours should match or beat? What do they do poorly that gives you an advantage?

Skip if: competitors were already covered in Batch 2, or if the business is niche/unique enough that local competition is not a strong factor.

---

### O.3 SEO priority topics

Ask if: local SEO seems important for this business type (home services, clinics, salons, fitness, any brick-and-mortar) and no keyword or search intent has been discussed.

> Are there specific services or searches you know your customers use to find businesses like yours? For example: "emergency plumber [city]," "best personal trainer near me," "wedding photographer [city]." Are there any search terms you are already ranking for that we should protect?

Skip if: the business is referral-only, not local, or the client has explicitly said SEO is out of scope.

---

### O.4 Seasonal or campaign context

Ask if: the business has a strong seasonal pattern or if there is a specific campaign or launch event that should influence the site's current focus.

> Is the business seasonal? Are you launching a specific offer, promotion, or campaign that the site should highlight right now? Is there a time window we are working toward?

Skip if: the business operates year-round without promotions or if no deadline has been mentioned.

---

### O.5 Social proof depth

Ask if: social proof was mentioned but the details are thin (e.g., "we have some reviews" without specifics).

> How many Google reviews do you have, and what is the average rating? Do you have 2–3 client testimonials with real names and results you can use? Do you have any before/after photos, case studies, or project results you can share?

Skip if: proof assets were already detailed in the required questions.

---

### O.6 Integrations and tools

Ask if: the build could realistically need third-party integrations and this has not been discussed.

> Are there any tools the site needs to connect with? For example: a booking system (Calendly, Acuity), a CRM (HubSpot, etc.), a payment processor (Stripe), a live chat tool, or an existing email list platform?

Skip if: the build is clearly a static informational site with just a contact form.

---

### O.7 Analytics and tracking requirements

Ask if: the client seems marketing-savvy or has mentioned measuring results.

> Do you want Google Analytics, Google Search Console, or any other tracking set up? Are there any conversion events that are important to track (form fills, button clicks, calls)?

Skip if: this is a basic first-time website for a client who shows no indication of caring about analytics beyond having a site.

---

### O.8 Accessibility and compliance

Ask if: the business type is regulated, serves vulnerable populations, or operates in a market where accessibility or legal compliance may be a factor.

> Are there accessibility requirements (WCAG compliance) or legal pages you need? For example: HIPAA-related disclaimers, Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, cookie consent?

Skip if: a simple local service business with no data collection or regulated industry context.

---

### O.9 Launch timeline and post-launch ownership

Ask if: no deadline or maintenance plan has been mentioned and this is needed to scope the build correctly.

> Is there a target launch date? After launch, who will update the site — the agency or the client? If the client, what level of editing ability do they have?

Note: if the client will edit the site themselves, flag that CMS consideration should be surfaced in `02-planning`.

---

## Tracking Which Optional Questions Were Asked

In the output brief, note in the Assumptions section which optional questions were skipped and why. Do not leave unexplained gaps.
