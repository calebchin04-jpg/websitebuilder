## 2. Business Context

La Grotta On Main is a family-owned Italian and Mediterranean restaurant located at 205 Main Street in historic Unionville, Ontario. Operated for 29 years, the restaurant is known for its warm atmosphere, signature dishes (rack of lamb, beef tenderloin, seafood), and a pet-friendly heated patio. The owner (Sebastian) is a recognized trust signal in customer reviews. The restaurant serves couples primarily, and also hosts private functions and events. Despite strong word-of-mouth and a 4.3★ Google rating, the current website does not communicate the family-owned character, warmth, or food quality that drives the in-person experience — creating a trust and perception gap at the digital point of decision.

## 3. Goals

**Primary site goal:** Drive phone reservation volume from couples in Unionville/Markham searching for an intimate Italian dining experience — by communicating the family-owned story, authentic food, and warm atmosphere compellingly enough that the visitor picks up the phone.

**Secondary goals:**

- Establish a stronger visual identity that matches the quality of the in-restaurant experience
- Improve mobile experience (currently poor)
- Build a gallery presence that showcases food, interior, patio, and events

## 4. Target Audience

**Primary audience:** Couples in Unionville/Markham area, likely 30–60, looking for a quality date-night Italian restaurant. They want atmosphere, authenticity, and confidence that the food and experience will be worth it. They are choosing between several options and the website is their deciding factor. They respond to story, warmth, and visual proof of quality — not just menu listings.

**Secondary audience:** Corporate or social groups organizing private functions and events in the Markham/Unionville area.

**What they are NOT:** Fast-casual diners, budget seekers, tourists looking for a quick lunch stop.

## 12. Visual Direction

**Overall feel:** Refined and warm. The site should feel like a well-run heritage restaurant — not trendy, not cold, not corporate. Clean typographic hierarchy with generous whitespace. Food and atmosphere photography does the emotional heavy lifting. Restrained use of gold as an accent against a black/white/neutral base. Movement is present but never distracts from the content.

**References provided:**
1. https://thebullandlast.co.uk/ — extract: whitespace generosity, typographic hierarchy, heritage storytelling structure, restrained CTA styling, section breathing room. Do NOT extract color scheme.

**Design principles extracted from references:**
- Whitespace is structural — sections breathe, nothing feels crowded or information-dense
- Typography does heavy lifting — clear size hierarchy, limited weights, no decorative overuse
- Photography is the hero — layout frames imagery, doesn't compete with it
- CTAs are calm and confident — no aggressive buttons, no high-contrast urgency patterns
- Heritage storytelling is woven into layout — not a separate "About" block, but present throughout
- No visual noise — each section has one clear job

**Banned styles / what to avoid:**
- Distracting animations or looping effects
- Busy background patterns or textures competing with content
- Oversized decorative script fonts as primary type
- Aggressive contrast CTAs (e.g., bright red or neon)
- Stock photography of generic people or food
- Cluttered layouts with too many competing elements per section

**Color constraints:**
- Brand colors: Black, white, gold/warm neutrals — keep existing palette
- Forbidden colors: None stated explicitly; avoid anything that clashes with the warm Italian palette
- Color direction: Warm — gold accent, neutral base, black for authority

**Font constraints:**
- Brand fonts: Not confirmed — to be determined in design system stage
- Font direction preference: Likely serif or refined sans-serif to match heritage restaurant positioning; avoid overly modern geometric sans

**Animation level:** Between subtle and moderate
- Definition: Scroll-triggered fade-ins and reveals on section entry. Smooth transitions. Possible subtle parallax on hero image. No looping animations, no auto-playing video, no animations that delay content access.

**Perceived quality and trust register:** Mid-premium local. Warmer and more personal than fine dining, more polished and credible than casual trattoria. The register is: "this is a real, special place run by real people" — not luxury, not chain.
