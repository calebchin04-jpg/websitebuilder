LA GROTTA ON MAIN — CLIENT ACTION ITEMS
=========================================
Everything below is needed before or during the build.
Items marked [BEFORE BUILD] are blockers — the build cannot be completed without them.
Items marked [BEFORE LAUNCH] can be provided later but must be done before the site goes live.
Items marked [OPTIONAL] improve the site but are not required.


CONTENT & COPY
--------------

1. [BEFORE BUILD] FOUNDING STORY
   We need a few sentences about the restaurant's history:
   - What year did La Grotta open? (We're estimating 1996 based on "29 years" — please confirm)
   - What was the original motivation for opening the restaurant?
   - What has stayed the same over the years?
   - Is the owner's name Sebastian? Is he comfortable being named/mentioned on the website?
   Even 3–5 sentences in your own words is enough — we'll turn it into polished copy.

2. [BEFORE BUILD] HOURS OF OPERATION
   Please provide the full weekly hours:
   - Monday through Sunday open/close times
   - Any seasonal variations or days closed

3. [BEFORE LAUNCH] MENU ACCURACY
   Is the menu currently on lagrottaonmain.ca up to date?
   - If yes, we'll build from that.
   - If no, please send the current menu (any format — photo, Word doc, PDF, handwritten is fine).

4. [BEFORE LAUNCH] PRIVATE FUNCTIONS DETAILS
   For the Private Functions page we need:
   - Maximum guest capacity for private events
   - Types of events you host (corporate dinners, birthdays, anniversaries, etc.)
   - Do you offer a set/fixed menu for private events? Any pricing you're comfortable disclosing?
   - Do you have AV or presentation equipment available?
   - What is the typical booking lead time you recommend?
   - These answers will be used to write the FAQ section on that page.


ASSETS
------

5. [BEFORE BUILD] LOGO FILE
   We need the logo in high resolution.
   - Preferred format: PNG or SVG with transparent background
   - If you don't have the file, we can attempt to re-export it from the current site — but a clean
     original file is much better.
   - Do you also have a white/reversed version of the logo (for dark backgrounds)?

6. [OPTIONAL] PROFESSIONAL PHOTOS
   We're sourcing photos from your Yelp listing and Instagram (@lagrottaonmain).
   If you have any professional food or interior photos saved on your phone or computer —
   even from a previous shoot — please send them. Higher resolution is always better.
   If photo quality turns out to be an issue, we may recommend a short professional
   photography session before launch.


TECHNICAL / SETUP
-----------------

7. [BEFORE BUILD] POST-LAUNCH EDITING PREFERENCE
   After the site is built, will you want to update content yourself (menu changes, photo updates,
   hours, etc.)?
   - YES → we need to add a content management system (CMS), which adds some build time
   - NO → we build a static site, which is simpler and faster
   Please let us know your preference.

8. [BEFORE BUILD] DOMAIN & HOSTING
   - Who currently hosts lagrottaonmain.ca and manages the domain?
   - Do you have login access to your hosting account?
   - We'll need to coordinate with you for the final deployment.

9. [OPTIONAL] GOOGLE BUSINESS PROFILE ACCESS
   If you have access to your Google Business Profile (the listing that shows up on Google Maps),
   it would help to ensure the profile and website are consistent.
   Not required, but useful.

10. [OPTIONAL] RESERVATION SYSTEM
    You currently take reservations by phone only. Is that how you'd like to keep it, or would
    you consider adding an online reservation system (like OpenTable or Resy) in the future?
    No action needed now — just useful to know for planning.


APPROVALS NEEDED DURING BUILD
------------------------------

11. Copy approval — we'll write all website copy and send it for your review before it goes live.
    Particularly important: the story/about section on the homepage.

12. Menu page review — once we build the menu page, please review it for accuracy before launch.

13. Private Functions page review — please confirm all event details and FAQ answers are accurate.
